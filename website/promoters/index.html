Wiji Promoter Programme

Share Wiji. Earn when people use it.

Get your own code and link. When the people you bring in pay for things in Wiji, or get paid through it, you earn a share of what Wiji earns. It costs you nothing, and there is nothing to buy or sell.

How it works

Three steps

  1. ApplyOpen Wiji, go to Profile, choose Promoter and tell us where you will share Wiji. You can also apply in the Wiji portal.
  2. Get your codeThe Wiji team checks your application and tells you in the app. Once approved you get your own code and link.
  3. Share and earnPeople enter your code when they sign up. When they pay for things in Wiji, you earn. You see everything in the app.

What you earn

20% of what Wiji earns

Wiji keeps a small fee on each payment in the app. You get 20% of that fee, after the payment company takes its own cut. You earn when real money is paid, never for sign-ups alone.

How long you earn

Two ways to earn, each with its own time

90 days

When they pay

Someone you brought in pays for a ride, an order or a group subscription. You earn on their payments for 90 days, counted from the first payment they make.

180 days

When they get paid

Someone you brought in is a driver, a business or a group creator and gets paid through Wiji. You earn on those payments for 180 days, counted from the first payment they receive.

The clock starts at their first payment, not when they sign up. When the time is up, you stop earning on that side. Everyone you bring in has their own clock.

Getting paid

Simple and safe

  • 30 days after the payment. A commission can be paid 30 days after the payment it came from, so that refunds are caught first. If a payment is refunded, its commission is cancelled.
  • R200 minimum. Once at least R200 is ready, we pay it by bank transfer to your South African bank account, normally once a month. You add the account in Profile, then Payouts.
  • You can see it all. The app shows each commission as waiting, ready to pay, paid, being checked or cancelled, and each payout we make.
  • Tax is yours. You are not an employee of Wiji. You are responsible for your own tax on what you earn.

The rules in short

Be honest and play fair

  • Say it is an ad. Add #ad or #sponsored to posts and videos where you share your code. This is what South African advertising rules expect.
  • Tell the truth. Do not promise earnings, rides or prices that Wiji has not promised, and do not say you work for Wiji.
  • No tricks. No fake accounts, no using your own code, no spam and no paying people to enter your code. We check commissions, and we cancel and may close accounts when we find cheating.
  • Keep people's information private. You can see the username, type of payment, date and amount for people who used your code. Use it only to understand your earnings.
  • 7 days to enter your code. A person must enter your code when they sign up, or within 7 days of creating their account. They can use only one code, once.

Questions

Good to know

Does it cost anything to join?

No. You do not pay to apply, and there is nothing to buy or stock to hold.

Do I earn when someone just downloads the app?

No. You earn only when a person you brought in pays for something in Wiji, or gets paid through it. Sign-ups alone earn nothing.

What if a person forgets to type my code?

They can add it within 7 days of creating their account. After that, it can no longer be added, so remind people when you share it.

Can I use my own code?

No. You also do not earn on payments where you are the other person in the payment.

Why was a commission held or cancelled?

A commission is held for a person on the Wiji team to check when something looks unusual, for example a payment made with your own card. It is cancelled if the payment is refunded or if we find cheating. The app tells you which, and you can write to us if you disagree.

Can the rules change?

Yes. We may change the rate, the time limits or the terms. We tell you in the app first, and a change applies only to payments made after the date we give.

Who do I ask if something looks wrong?

Email molefempho783@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 5 working days.
