Wiji brings the apps you jump between every day into one place, and keeps safety at the centre of every ride.

Rides, built around safety

Gender-based violence makes getting into a stranger's car a real risk. Wiji is designed so that every passenger, and every driver, knows who they are travelling with.

  • Every driver is checked by our safety team: ID, licence, PrDP, car papers and a criminal record check, renewed every year.
  • A trip PIN makes sure you get into the right car. If something doesn't match, tap "Not my driver".
  • Share your trip live with trusted contacts.
  • An emergency button alerts your contacts and our team, with your location.
  • See the fare before you go. The price you see is the price you pay.

Pay and get paid

Pay drivers, shops and friends by card or by scanning a QR code. Payments are processed securely by Paystack and land in the payee's bank account.

Shop local, find deals

Order from verified local businesses and find specials near you on the map, from lunch deals to weekend events.
